Inventory

Manually track stock. Quantities are not deducted automatically by orders, and inventory changes do not create finance entries.

Download the workbook, edit the Inventory sheet and upload it again. Imports replace quantities and details for matching item codes, add new items and leave other items unchanged. Supplier IDs must already exist. Maximum 1,000 items per workbook.
